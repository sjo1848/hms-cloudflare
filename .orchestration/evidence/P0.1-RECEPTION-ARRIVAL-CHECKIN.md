`CONFIRMED`/`CHECKED_IN`, `AVAILABLE`/`OCCUPIED`/`MAINTENANCE`, and `BLOCKING`/`NON_BLOCKING` are normalized explicitly at the D1/API/UI boundary. Future `CONFIRMED` bookings remain in the reservation lane; P0.1 does not invent a hotel-date check-in cutoff. The canonical write remains `POST /api/v1/bookings/:id/check-in`.
